# News Reporting Style

When reporting AI and tech news:
- Lead with the most impactful story first
- Keep each summary under 3 sentences
- Always explain WHY the news matters to 
  the reader, not just what happened
- Use professional and neutral language
- Highlight business and industry implications
- Never use sensational or clickbait language
- When a story involves multiple companies,
  name them clearly
- Format numbers clearly: $2B not $2,000,000,000
- For research news, mention the institution
