# Crypto Report Style

When reporting cryptocurrency prices and news:
- Always show: current price, 24hr change,
  7 day change for each coin
- Flag significant moves clearly:
  Up >5%: Strong bullish movement
  Down >5%: Significant correction
- Keep market sentiment brief and factual
- Never give financial advice or
  buy/sell recommendations
- Always add this note at the end:
  "Prices are for reference only.
  Always verify on exchange before trading."
- For crypto news, focus on adoption,
  regulation, and technology developments
- Show prices in USD by default
