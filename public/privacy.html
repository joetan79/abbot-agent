ABbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

ABbot is a private Telegram assistant run by its owner for personal and family use.

Google Calendar data

  • ABbot accesses Google Calendar only for the owner's own account, after the owner explicitly authorizes it.
  • It reads events to answer the owner's questions (for example "what's on my calendar today") and creates or changes events only when the owner asks.
  • The OAuth token is stored only on ABbot's private server. Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • To answer requests, event text may be processed by the AI model provider that powers ABbot (Anthropic).

Revoking access

You can remove ABbot's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
